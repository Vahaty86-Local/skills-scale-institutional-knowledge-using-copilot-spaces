# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository documents OctoAcme's project management approach for cross-functional product, service, and integration work. The process is customer-first, iterative, data-informed, and built around clear ownership and collaboration.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme moves work through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Teams validate the business need and measurable success criteria, align stakeholders, and confirm team availability before moving into planning. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, owners, milestones, a Definition of Done, and plans for risks and dependencies.

During execution, teams use a project board to track work from Backlog to Done, hold regular standups and delivery syncs, review small pull requests, run automated tests and linting, and escalate blockers through defined team, PM, product, and sponsor levels. The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, documentation, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes and prioritizes work; Developers implement, test, and review changes; and QA/Testing validates quality and feature acceptance.

Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, prepared release notes and smoke tests, a rollback or mitigation plan, and post-deployment verification. Communication remains regular and transparent through weekly status updates, stakeholder reports, a shared source of truth, and project dashboards. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons and convert them into owned, dated improvement actions that are tracked in the backlog and reviewed in subsequent syncs.
